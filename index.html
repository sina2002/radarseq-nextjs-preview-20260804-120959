Overview

Snapshots miss what sequences reveal.

1

Trajectory, not snapshot

RadarSeq reads the path a signal takes over time, not a single point-in-time score — so a slow drift toward risk is visible long before it becomes a loss.

2

Five-stage radar

Every entity is placed on a five-stage scale — Stable, Drift, Squeeze, Stress, Distress — so a portfolio's risk position is legible at a glance.

3

Explainable by design

Every prediction carries a full audit trail back to the signals that produced it, built for review, not just for accuracy.

Innovations

Three ideas most risk models skip.

01

Sequence-aware encoding

Behavioural history is encoded as an ordered sequence, so the model learns how risk builds, not just what it looks like at one moment.

02

Stage-transition modelling

RadarSeq models the transitions between risk stages directly, capturing momentum that a single classification score discards.

03

Cross-source fusion

Signals from separate systems — transactions, engagement, support, payments — are fused into one continuous risk trajectory.

Applied Research

Case study — predicting courier churn with a small LLM.

By Soham Patel, University of East London

Gig-platform couriers churn with no warning, costing $8,000–$10,000 to replace each one. This study fine-tunes a small open-source language model to read a courier's daily work history and predict 45-day churn — grounded in three published methods (TALLRec, BehaviorLM, CoLLM) before a line of code was written.

create_seq.py

Daily logs → conversation

for day in courier.history:
    prompt = summarize(day.orders,
                        day.acceptance_rate,
                        day.earnings)
    dataset.append(to_chat_pair(prompt))
Sample exchange
You: Orders down 34% this week, acceptance rate fell from 91% to 68%, earnings down 21%. What's the churn risk?
AI: High churn risk. The combined drop in acceptance rate and order volume mirrors the pre-churn pattern seen in the training set — recommend proactive outreach within 3 days.
Credibility

Backed by published research.

RadarSeq is grounded in the peer-reviewed ACM/Springer 2025 paper "RadarSeq: A Temporal Vision Framework for User Churn Prediction via Radar Chart Sequences" by Najafi, Sepanj and Jafari, validated on roughly 16,000 couriers and outperforming CatBoost, ChurnViT, CNN-only and CNN+MLP baselines.
